Chapter 01 / The Story

A Life Less
Ordinary.

I've always been fascinated by what's possible.

I'm Scott Stoneburner — an entrepreneur, creative, collector, and lifelong student of the human experience.

My journey has taken me through banking, technology, biotechnology, business, entrepreneurship, art, music, and photography. Along the way, I've discovered that some of life's most meaningful experiences happen when we venture beyond what's familiar.

I'm a United States Air Force veteran, an MBA graduate, and someone who has spent much of his life exploring ideas, building businesses, connecting people, and pursuing new opportunities.

Some ideas become businesses. Others become lessons. Some turn into photographs, music, friendships, or unexpected adventures.

I believe in curiosity, individual liberty, lifelong learning, and the freedom to create a life that reflects who we are.
