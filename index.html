01 / The Person

A Life Less
Ordinary.

I've always been fascinated by what's possible.

I'm Scott Stoneburner — an entrepreneur, creative, collector, and lifelong student of the human experience.

My path has crossed banking, technology, biotechnology, entrepreneurship, photography, art and music.

I'm a United States Air Force veteran and an MBA graduate, but those are only a few chapters of the story.

I've followed ideas that became businesses, ideas that didn't, and ideas that led somewhere entirely unexpected.

Each has taught me something about curiosity, resilience and the value of beginning.

I believe life is richer when we keep learning, making things, meeting people and staying open to possibility.

— Scott
