01 / About

A Life of
Curiosity & Creation.

I'm Scott Stoneburner — an entrepreneur, creative, collector, and lifelong student of the human experience.

My interests have taken me through business, banking, technology, biotechnology, art, music, photography, and entrepreneurship.

I've always been fascinated by the intersection of ideas, people, and opportunity — and by what happens when curiosity becomes creation.

Whether I'm building a business, discovering new music, photographing the world, connecting artists, or exploring a new idea, I'm drawn to the same thing: the possibility of creating something meaningful.

I'm also a United States Air Force veteran and hold an MBA. Those experiences helped shape my approach to leadership, discipline, and entrepreneurship.

Beyond the work, I'm a believer in individual liberty, lifelong learning, and the importance of living deliberately.
